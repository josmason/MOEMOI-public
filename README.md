## What this is

MOEMOI is an open, testable framework built around a single claim: every intelligent system, from a cell to a person to a civilisation, runs the same underlying process to get from where it is to somewhere better.

That process is the **Intelligence Process (IP)**: a loop comprising six substeps (Experience, Understand, Prioritise, Define, Prepare, Do/Learn; abbreviated EUPDDL), with a conditional multi-agent substep (Communicate) that activates when the loop crosses between minds.

The framework's contribution is not inventing this loop; it was already there in the scientific method, in decision theory, in how good practitioners in every field actually work. The contribution is naming it once, making it teachable, grounding each step in evidence, and applying it deliberately across a whole life.

## The core claim (and its epistemic status)

**Master claim (v1.3, confidence ~0.85):** The function of all intelligence is to continuously close the gap between its current state and a more optimal state, recursively, by running one shared process.

*Epistemic posture:* this is a strong operational claim held with high confidence, not a philosophical assertion held with certainty. Claims are tracked in the Claims Register with individual confidence levels, status (HYPOTHESIS / CANDIDATE / ADOPTED / ARCHIVED / HELD), and supporting evidence. Where the framework goes beyond the evidence, it says so.

*Graph-structural note (B24621):* A reference-graph analysis across the 132 sequential claims (C1-C132) at the time of the study confirms that the IP loop specification (C20, CANDIDATE) is the framework's functional hub -- combined graph degree 82, equal in- and out-degree (41/41) -- not the philosophical master claim C1 (degree 16). The operational architecture is the load-bearing structure; C1 provides the philosophical umbrella. Researchers inspecting the claims register should expect C20 and C59 (IP refinements, ADOPTED) to carry the highest cross-claim dependency weight. (C133-C143 added subsequently; the hub structure is stable -- C20 remains highest-degree at the current C1-C145 range.)

**Falsifiability (short version):** The primary test is operational: a practitioner who systematically runs the IP loop with high fidelity should outperform matched controls on outcome measures in health, work, and relationships. A first-principles derivation arriving at a *different* loop architecture with equal logical support, or a well-powered trial showing IP-completeness scores do not predict outcomes, would require revision of the master claim. The framework is designed to be disconfirmed by its own evidence standard; a full statement of falsification conditions is in the [Falsifiability section](#falsifiability) below and in the preprint (Section 7).

## Key structural ideas

**The loop always has the same shape.** Experience, Understand, Prioritise, Define, Prepare, Do/Learn. Every iteration run well closes a gap. Every iteration skimped leaves an open loop that costs the next one. The loop can be run on anything: a health problem, a creative project, a relationship, a business.

**Gaps are the unit of currency.** The framework does not specify what you should want; it specifies how to close whatever gap you have, more completely and with less unnecessary cost. The choosing is yours.

**Suffering is a byproduct, not the target.** Reducing unnecessary suffering is what falls out when you close gaps well. Targeting suffering reduction directly often produces avoidance, which keeps gaps open. The prescriptions point at loop fidelity, not at emotional states.

**The framework is person-independent.** It is not built around one person's life, values, or preferences. It is built to be testable: if running the loop well does not produce better outcomes, the framework is wrong, and the claims register should be updated.

## How this differs from other frameworks and AI tools

Most productivity systems tell you what to do. Most AI tools execute tasks for you. MOEMOI does something structurally different on four dimensions:

**G1: Measurement flywheel.** Every run of the IP loop generates a fidelity score: how completely was each substep run? The score feeds back into the next run. Most frameworks have no native measurement; MOEMOI's claims register and per-run scoring are designed so the framework can be evaluated and revised against evidence, including evidence that something in it is wrong.

**G2: The human half of the loop.** Habits, practices, and perception tools for running the IP substeps well (not just tracking them) are built into the system. The AI handles the data and orchestration; the practitioner handles the lived experience. Both halves are necessary; neither replaces the other.

**G3: The Define cap.** The practitioner retains full authority over what they are trying to close gaps toward. The framework deliberately does not specify what you should want or value. It specifies how to close whatever gap you have. This is not an accident; it is a design commitment.

**G4: The meta-layer.** The system runs the IP on itself: it tracks its own adherence, scores its own outputs, and updates its own instructions when it finds a better approach. This is bounded recursive self-improvement at the operating-system level, not a claim about AGI.

**vs. Platform Personal AI (Microsoft Windows 11, 2026):** Microsoft's repositioning of Windows 11 as a "Personal AI OS" (MXC containers, persistent on-device memory, OpenClaw agent interop standard) confirms that the personal-AI operating system category is real at mass-market scale. The contrast with MOEMOI is structural: Windows 11 Personal AI operates at the infrastructure and action layer: OS-locked, vendor-owned, and memory that is on-device but Microsoft-controlled. MOEMOI operates at the goal-management and reasoning-quality layer: voluntary, model-agnostic, open, and principled by the IP loop rather than by behavioural trace capture. The two are not competing for the same user in the same way: Windows 11 is the default environment; MOEMOI is a voluntary overlay for practitioners who want to run the IP deliberately. Note: OpenClaw (Microsoft's adopted agent interop standard) is the same local-agent gateway protocol already used in MOEMOI's federation architecture, which means federation agents built on OpenClaw-compatible interfaces will be interoperable with the Windows 11 ecosystem by default. *[NX-B27433, CANDIDATE 0.68, 1 Aug 2026]*

**vs. Agent OS abstraction layer (IBM Research framing, arXiv:2607.25076):** IBM Research (Steinder & Franke, Jul 2026) frames the current moment in AI as an "agentic AI experimentation phase" that is generating demand for a universal cognitive abstraction layer — analogous to how POSIX standardised Unix or how Kubernetes standardised container orchestration. Their prediction: one dominant abstraction for how agents reason and coordinate will emerge, just as one dominant abstraction emerged at each prior infrastructure layer. MOEMOI's IP loop is a candidate at precisely this cognitive layer: a universal six-step process claim (Experience, Understand, Prioritise, Define, Prepare, Do/Learn) backed by a graded claims register specifying where the process is universal (substrate-independent) and where it is personalised per practitioner. The key distinction from infrastructure-layer Agent OS proposals: MOEMOI is not an API standard, a runtime protocol, or an OS component. It operates above the runtime layer at the level of how any specific agent — human or AI — closes the gap between where it is and where it is trying to go. The runtime layer (Windows 11, cloud orchestration) routes and executes; the IP loop specifies what to run and why. If the Steinder–Franke prediction is correct and a universal cognitive abstraction does emerge, MOEMOI's claims register provides the falsifiable specification against which candidates in that space can be evaluated.

## The claims register

165 claims (C1-C145 sequential + aliased claims; v0.15-v0.38 folds, 7 Aug 2026), 82 ADOPTED. Each claim has:
- A unique C-number or aliased ID
- A status: Hypothesis, Candidate, Adopted, or Archived
- A confidence level (0–1)
- Supporting evidence and update history

The 57 CANDIDATE claims are not unfinished work. They are pre-registered hypotheses with explicit kill-lines: a specific result that would require them to be downgraded. Falsifying a CANDIDATE claim is a contribution to the framework, not an attack on it. The 82 ADOPTED claims have passed the stated evidence threshold and are treated as working assumptions. The 22 HYPOTHESIS claims are well-formed conjectures that remain open to evidence from any direction.

This is the mechanism that keeps the framework honest. A confident-sounding claim that fails its evidence standard gets downgraded, not quietly defended. The register is what separates MOEMOI from a book of advice.

## Falsifiability

The framework makes operationally testable predictions. The primary one: a measurable completeness score for how fully the IP loop was run should predict outcomes in health, work, relationships, and energy; better than time-on-task, intention, or effort alone. This has not been run as a controlled trial yet. The early n=1 data (one practitioner, daily tracking) is directional but insufficient. The framework says: if the completeness score does not predict outcomes when properly tested, the master claim needs revision.

## Open research problems

The claims register surfaces five tractable research problems that independent researchers could pursue. Each includes the relevant claims and their current epistemic status.

**RP1 — Completeness score predictive validity (empirical, pre-registered)**
The framework's central prediction: practitioners who score higher on IP-completeness measures should outperform matched controls on outcomes in health, work, and relationships. No adequately-powered trial has been run. An n>=20 within-subjects design with daily IP-scoring over 8 weeks and outcome measures at 30/60/90 days would test the operational core of the master claim. Positive result: promotes C20 (CANDIDATE) toward ADOPTED. Null or negative result: requires revision of C1 (ADOPTED 0.85).
*Claims: C1 (ADOPTED 0.85), C20 (CANDIDATE), C59 (ADOPTED).*

**RP2 — Universal substrate independence (formal/empirical)**
The framework asserts the IP loop applies to cells, persons, organisations, and AI without substrate modification. Formal route: derive whether a minimal loop-completion structure is provably necessary for any goal-directed optimisation problem, independent of substrate. Empirical route: identify a goal-directed system that demonstrably does not run any analogue of all six steps. Either result — proof or counterexample — advances the claim.
*Claims: C1, C15 (CANDIDATE), C95 (CANDIDATE).*

**RP3 — Claims-register methodology as a scientific communication standard**
The framework uses 165 individual falsifiable claims rather than a single global hypothesis. Research question: does a claims-register format produce better epistemic updating over time than conventional paper format? Measurable proxies: rate of ghost-claim persistence, speed of error correction after disconfirmation, cumulative evidence integration across studies. The register in this repo is an existence proof; a comparative study of its updating behaviour vs a matched set of conventional papers would be the test.
*Claims: C73 (ADOPTED — cumulative evidence integration is more reliable than individual study replication).*

**RP4 — Recursive self-improvement at the harness layer: convergence with 2026 AI architectures**
Three independent 2026 papers (AREX arXiv:2607.21461, CogniFold arXiv:2605.13438, RHI arXiv:2607.15524) describe outer loops that improve an agent's scaffold without weight modification. MOEMOI's canonical-fold operation is the proposed human-practitioner analogue: the worklog (episodic traces) compresses into the canonical (semantic abstraction) via a fold step, preserving verified evidence and unresolved constraints. Research question: is there a formal equivalence between harness-level BRSI in these AI systems and the MOEMOI canonical-fold architecture? If so, C43 (BRSI, ADOPTED 0.90) may be derivable rather than empirical.
*Claims: C43 (ADOPTED 0.90 -- bounded recursive self-improvement), C138 (ADOPTED 0.83 -- fold-cadence is architecturally load-bearing).*

**RP5 — Void taxonomy as a diagnostic instrument**
The framework classifies recurring gaps into six void types: five deficit voids (epistemic, conditional, competence, relational, autonomy) and one generative drive. A validated diagnostic instrument derived from this taxonomy would allow cross-domain comparison of which void types are most prevalent and most tractable across different populations. Grounding is in SDT, Maslow, and Loewenstein (cited in the claims register). The gap is a validated measurement instrument; a psychometric development study with exploratory and confirmatory factor analysis would be the entry point.
*Claims: C6 (ADOPTED — void taxonomy; confidence 0.78), C7-C12 (individual void types, ADOPTED/CANDIDATE).*

## Known limitations

- The practitioner prescriptions are tested in a single practitioner (n=1) and have not been validated across diverse cultural or demographic contexts. All n=1 conclusions are hypothesis-generating, not confirmatory.
- The framework has not yet been tested via a pre-registered comparative trial with adequate statistical power. The structural derivations are strong; the gap between "theoretically compelling" and "empirically confirmed" remains open.
- The CODEACCI trend claim (C43, ADOPTED, confidence 0.90) crossed the adoption threshold 1 Aug 2026 via the IIC analytical resolution (LR ~1.15; fire105-THEORY B30101). Note on precision: the Consciousness (C) dimension in CODEACCI has been analytically resolved under the information-integration capacity (IIC) reading — C is derivable from the Complexity dimension via IIT/GWT (Tononi 2004, 2008), making the independent empirical core of C43 {Complexity, Order, Evolvability, Intelligence}. Additionally, Diversity (D) has been formally reclassified as a phase-characteristic (exploration/exploitation enabling condition, non-directional; B25801, 30 Jul 2026), and Automation-terminal (A) is analytically derived from Intelligence and Complexity-Order rather than constituting an independent substrate trend (B25531, 30 Jul 2026). The operational claims do not require resolving the phenomenal-consciousness question at all.
- The multi-agent signal layer (Section 6 of the preprint) is preliminary. The prescriptions are neurologically motivated but have not been tested as a practitioner-level intervention.
- The four-level obstacle taxonomy (Section 2.6 of the preprint) now includes biological and neurological constraints as Level 4 (C137, ADOPTED 0.82; v0.21 fold, 1 Aug 2026). Practitioners with chronic illness, neurological differences, or metabolic conditions affecting executive function should engage qualified clinical support alongside any LOS practices; the Level 4 carve-out prescribes clinical routing as the primary intervention for this obstacle class.

## Discussion and contributions

Questions, critiques, and proposals for new evidence are welcome. The claims register is the mechanism: if you have evidence that changes a claim's confidence, raise it as an issue with the relevant C-number.

The framework is intended to be tested against evidence, including evidence that part of it is wrong. Falsifying a claim is a contribution, not an attack.
